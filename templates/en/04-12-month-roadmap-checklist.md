A rejection here is a result, not a failure. Record it as one.

## Three Principles for When You Get Stuck

Pin this part somewhere you'll see it.

1. **Don't fix by guessing — read the current state first.** Changing this and that without knowing
   what's wrong only blurs the cause further. Look at the log, the screen, the error message first,
   exactly as they are.
2. **When you fix, fix a little at a time, reversibly.** Change several places at once and you lose
   track of what made it better and what made it worse.
3. **If you get stuck, show Claude the screen as-is, the whole error message.** Don't summarize —
   copy and paste the original text verbatim. The original is always more accurate than you
   describing "an error that felt roughly like this."
